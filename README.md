# 鴉片煙之害（約1880–1890年漢口印單，牛津 Sinica 6531） / Ya pian yan zhi hai [The Harms of Opium Smoking]: Transcription Dataset

《鴉片煙之害》（Ya pian yan zhi hai）是漢口聖教書局印發的戒鴉片單張，館目推定刊於1880至1890年間。十一條分述吸食在作者看來如何耗費家計與時間、影響身體與日常責任。此件保存晚清聖教書局的公共衛生勸戒話語與單張版面。

Ya pian yan zhi hai is a Chinese anti-opium broadside issued by the Religious Tract Society in Hankou, provisionally dated by the Bodleian catalogue to 1880–1890. Eleven numbered arguments discuss the reported effects of opium smoking on finances, time, the body, and everyday obligations. The source documents the language and graphic organization of late Qing public-health advocacy.

## V0.2.0

One recto scan containing the printed title, imprint, and eleven sections. Later English pencil text, shelfmark, and the circular collection stamp are separate. Independent local OCR candidates were compared with the initial transcription. Targeted source-image review adopted 3 corrections. Some readings, small notes, and reading marks still need review.

## 方法與覆核 / Methods and review

由 AI 依固定館方影像逐欄讀字，保留原字、版面與疑讀。保留標題與小字欄順，刻點以頓號表示，後加手書與藏印另列。人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查及準確率量測尚未進行。實際模型識別與使用量未知。此資料公開供比對與引用，處理程式及影像另存。

An AI assistant transcribed the fixed source images column by column, preserving source forms, layout and uncertain readings. Headings and small-type columns follow the physical order; punctuation marks use ideographic commas, and later marks are separate. Human proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement are pending. The actual model identifiers and usage are unknown.
